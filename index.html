About

Architecture graduate from the University of Auckland, interested in the intersection of spatial design, interactive technology, and immersive storytelling. My master’s thesis developed a room-scale responsive environment where physical space senses and responds to occupants in real time.

Two years as a Teaching Assistant at the Digital Research Hub, developing self-initiated interactive and generative projects alongside supporting students in digital fabrication, design software, and creative workflows.

Spatial Design & Built Environment

Projects exploring various types of architecture — from community housing and urban planning to retail interiors and spatial prototyping.

The Living Space

A room-scale prototype where everyday objects become spatially aware companions — sensing position, detecting interaction, and remembering conversation. Each object responds in real time through voice generated by a large language model from its own perspective, accompanied by pre-designed projected visuals.
